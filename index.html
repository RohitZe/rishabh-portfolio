// about

Hi, I'm Rishabh.

I train people, not just deliver content. Most data science courses dump theory and hope it sticks. I build sessions backwards from the questions a working analyst or ML engineer actually has to answer on the job — what to measure, which model is overkill, when the data is lying to you.

My background spans statistics, applied machine learning, and analytics consulting, but the part I care about most is the classroom — turning confused beginners into people who can open a messy dataset and know exactly where to start.

When I'm not teaching, I'm mentoring early-career analysts and writing about the gap between Kaggle notebooks and production data science.
